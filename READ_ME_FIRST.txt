KRANGLE CAPITAL — your party app
=================================

BEFORE IT WORKS: open config.js and paste in your two Supabase values
(Project URL + anon public key). That's the only edit you ever make.
You do NOT need the secret/service_role key anywhere — keep it private.

The pages:
  index.html        -> friendly landing ("tap your card")
  card.html?id=KC-001 .. KC-040  -> each guest's account (this is what NFC cards open)
  leaderboard.html  -> the TV screen
  admin.html        -> your host controls (it'll ask you to set a password the first time)

Follow the chat for the click-by-click "put it online" steps.
Together We Sleigh.
